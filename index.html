What
we do

We live in a time of psychedelic renaissance. The healing potential of these medicines was known to our ancestors for thousands of years, then lost and almost forgotten until the late 1950s, when R. Gordon Wasson brought the first “magic mushrooms” from Mexico to the US. Since then this sacred medicine has played a big role in Western civilization.

Our mission is to assist and guide people on their path. We use natural entheogens along with mindful practices such as yoga, meditation, breathwork, ecstatic dance and chanting to support and enhance the healing process.

Services

With gentle physical exercise, breathing techniques, meditation and a yoga lifestyle, we help people connect with their inner self, receive the most from the healing process and smoothly integrate it back into daily life.

Yoga Classes

A range of yoga classes, including Hatha, Vinyasa and Kundalini, taught by experienced yoga teachers.

Mindfulness & Meditation Workshops

Workshops and classes focused on mindfulness and meditation, including guided meditations and mindfulness-based stress reduction (MBSR) practices.

Retreats

A wide range of services for your retreat: sitting and facilitating, individual and group yoga programs, mentorship, group integration classes and more.

Aftercare Support

Support and resources to help you maintain your mental health and wellness after sessions.

Retreats

Small-group retreats where daily yoga and guided psychedelic experience are woven together.

  1. Prepare

    Yoga, breathwork and conversation to settle the body and set an intention.

  2. Journey

    A guided session in a calm, supported setting, with a sitter present throughout.

  3. Integrate

    Movement, rest and group integration to carry what you found into daily life.

Pasha

Founder · RYT200 Yoga Instructor · Psychedelic Assisted Retreat Sitter & Facilitator

My journey began back in 2016, when I first encountered psilocybin mushrooms, which completely changed the course of my life. The sacred mushrooms helped me to realize, accept and heal my own traumas and finally accept myself “as I am”.

Today many people tend to believe in a magic pill, and consider sacred medicine the one that will heal them in a single session without much effort. That is why I started Yoga Cybin. I strongly believe that classical yoga, such as the Ashtanga Yoga system, can do more than prepare people for a deeper, safer and more meaningful experience. It also gives journeyers a set of tools and knowledge that stays with them and helps them heal over time, without constantly relying on therapists, more retreats and “healers”.
