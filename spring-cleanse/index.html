Cleanse

Day 4 The cleanse begins

The formal cleanse starts today. In your morning practice, light a candle for everyone on the cleanse.

Food

  • Breakfast only if you are hungry: a small, warm, well-cooked meal. Otherwise tea, golden milk or a green drink.
  • Cut out processed foods, sugar, coffee, alcohol, recreational drugs, soda and any food you know you are allergic to.
  • No sweeteners except local honey. Apple juice is fine.

Morning

  • Optional oleation: 1–2 tablespoons of melted ghee, after your warm water and before sadhana.
  • If you oleate, do Agni Sara during sadhana.
  • Sadhana: your own yoga, pranayama and meditation.

Body care

  • Start daily abhyanga (self-massage with oil), before or after your bath or shower.

Plus the daily routine and plenty of rest.

Daily routine

Every day of the cleanse, from Day 4. The order of the morning steps can vary.

Morning

  1. Wake early, ideally at sunrise.
  2. First thought: a prayer, mantra or visualization for one minute, such as “24 brand new hours”.
  3. Eliminate, and observe. Urine is ideally clear and medium yellow. Notice undigested food or an unusually strong smell.
  4. Oil pulling, brush teeth, scrape tongue. How
  5. Warm water: one cup or less, purified, never cold. Lemon or lime if you like.
  6. Oleation on Days 4–7. How
  7. Sadhana: yoga, pranayama, then meditation, prayer or chanting. More
  8. Bath or shower with abhyanga. How

Through the day

  • Rest. This one is not optional. Your body, mind and spirit are going through a gentle but deep transformation. Do not schedule a lot of work or socializing, especially during the deep cleanse. Nap and put your legs up the wall when you need to.
  • Neti, nasya, time in nature, singing, emotional work. See the practice guide

Evening

  • Screens off by 9 pm or earlier. Dim the lights or use candles.
  • Wind down: read, gentle yoga, meditation, trataka, journaling, legs up the wall, yoga nidra, prayer, counting blessings, time with family.
  • In bed by 10. Lengthen your exhalations as you lie down.
  • Reduce or pause sexual activity, and fast from violent or disturbing films, news and images.

Core practice Optional

Food basics

  • Home-cooked, fresh, seasonal, organic, local, real food.
  • Mineral-rich salt; no refined oils.
  • No snacking between meals.
  • Sip warm or hot water, CCF tea (cumin, coriander, fennel) or herbal teas through the day. Tulsi, brahmi and detox teas are great.
  • Avoid nightshades. Favor root vegetables, peas, asparagus, greens and sprouts.
  • Try eating in silence, as a meditation.

Breakfast

Skip it if you are not hungry. Otherwise keep it small, warm and well cooked: oatmeal, cooked buckwheat, or eggs if you need protein (before Day 5). For sweetness use dates, prunes or honey; prunes stewed in water with cinnamon are excellent. Avoid salsa, sugar, cold cereal, frozen foods and smoothies that mix fruit and vegetables.

Kitchari

The staple of the cleanse: rice and split mung beans cooked soft with ghee and mild spices. For spring, cook in sprouts, beets, asparagus, dandelion or other bitter greens, which support the liver and get bile moving. Serve with plenty of ghee and fresh cilantro.

Green drink

Cucumber, celery, apple, ginger, lemon or lime, and cilantro or parsley. Nothing frozen. “Chew” it rather than gulping it down.

Shopping

Ghee and kitchari ingredients are sold by Banyan Botanicals, Natural Grocers and Indian markets such as Bombay Bazaar. For ghee, choose a pure one or make it yourself from organic butter. Herbs are available from Banyan Botanicals, Natural Grocers, Apothecary Tinctura and other shops around Denver.

Practice guide

Oleation Optional · Days 4–7

Drinking melted ghee (or coconut or flax oil) lubricates the channels of the body so that ama, or toxicity, can release more easily. Take it in the morning, about 10 minutes after your warm water, on Days 4 to 7, raising the amount each day. If you have trouble digesting it, add a pinch of dry ginger and black pepper.

Do not do oleation if you do not have a gallbladder.

To reduce Kapha and Pitta you can use Tikta ghee instead (be warned, it is very bitter) or take 2 neem tablets with your oleation.

Agni Sara With oleation

A kriya that massages the abdominal organs. Do 3 repetitions during sadhana, on an empty stomach and about 10 minutes after oleation. Superb during a cleanse.

Watch the Agni Sara technique

Sadhana: yoga, pranayama, meditation Every day

Your spiritual practice, every day of the cleanse. Choose your own yoga, breathwork and meditation, prayer or chanting. If Ayurvedic yoga is new to you, read this introduction or ask Pasha for guidance.

Abhyanga (oil massage) Recommended

Self-massage with oil: anoint your sacred temple. Strongly recommended for everyone, especially with high Vata. Do it in the morning or evening, classically before your bath or shower, or afterwards if you would rather keep oil out of the tub.

On the sweating days you can go deeper with castor oil before swedana, as described in this spring self-care guide.

Garshana (dry brushing) Days 10–13

Dry brushing exfoliates, moves lymph, reduces ama and improves circulation. Before your bath or shower, brush the dry skin to bring blood to the surface. You do not need silk gloves or a special brush: a rough old towel or exfoliating gloves work. A must if you have a lot of Kapha imbalance or ama; in that case follow it with castor oil abhyanga.

Watch the garshana technique

Swedana (sweating) Days 10–13

Purging through the skin. If you fast, sweat each day of the deep cleanse plus one more day. Use a sauna or simply a hot bath, for no more than 20 minutes, and drink plenty of water afterwards. If you run very Pitta, wrap your head in a cold towel.

For more heat and drawing power, add to the bath ¼ cup ginger powder, ¼ cup baking soda and 1 cup Epsom salt. Skip the ginger if you are very Pitta.

Triphala Days 7–10

Taken as a tea 1 hour before bed on Days 7 to 10: 1 teaspoon of powder in 1 cup of water, or 2 tablets. Reduce the dose if you get diarrhea or cramping. Available at Natural Grocers or from Banyan Botanicals.

Basti Optional · Days 12–16

A warm, oily, medicated enema, excellent for dispelling Vata from the lower body, especially aches in the legs, hips and low back. You can skip the medicated part (dashamula, or ten-roots tea) and use just warm water and sesame oil. Do it in the evening, twice in total, and take a probiotic food afterwards.

Please read the general information and contraindications first, and ask Pasha for step-by-step instructions.

Rasayana Days 14–18

Rasayanas, or rejuvenatives, help you rebuild ojas after a cleanse. From Day 14, take one warm in the evening, an hour before bed or in place of dinner, and continue for a week after the cleanse ends. Choose one:

  • An ojas-building shake. Add turmeric and ginger to make it more like golden milk.
  • Chyavanprash, a sweet, spicy herbal jam (it contains cane sugar). Blend a heaped teaspoon into warm milk until frothy.
  • Golden milk.
Other herbal support Optional

Herbs add benefit, but also cost and complexity. Milk thistle is welcome throughout, and malic acid through Day 9, for liver support. Medicinal mushrooms, nettles, oatstraw, horsetail, mint, cloves, anise and chamomile are all good.

Manjistha, ashwagandha, shatavari, tulsi and brahmi are excellent too; ask Pasha for specifics on these.

Oil pulling and tongue scraping Optional

Swish oil in your mouth while you make the bed, wash the dishes or stretch, then spit it out (do not swallow) and brush your teeth. Scrape your tongue afterwards; a spoon with a slightly sharp edge works if you do not have a scraper. Morning or evening, any day.

Neti pot and nasya Optional

Neti: add a little salt to purified, slightly warm water and flush each nostril to clean the sinus passages.

Nasya: put a few drops of coconut or sesame oil, or a herbal nasya oil, into the nostrils. Can be done daily.

Emotional work Optional

Write a letter, have a conversation, forgive someone, make amends, set a boundary, let go. Even if you do not choose this, emotions are likely to surface during the cleanse. Take good care of them: acknowledge them, thank them and let them flow through you.

Nature and sound Optional

Lean against a tree, lie on the earth, soak up the sun, walk barefoot, do a slow walking meditation, gaze at the moon, work the soil.

Sing, hum or practise Bhramari breath: a strong vibration in the upper body tones the vagus nerve.

More ideas Optional

Clean a closet, wash out the fridge, pray before meals, create a bedtime ritual, journal, play an instrument, chant, practise japa, bathe with Epsom salts, make art, dance, plant seeds. What else?

Take care

This program is a guide, not medical advice. Fasting and cleansing practices are not right for everyone. If you are pregnant or nursing, take medication, or have diabetes, a history of disordered eating or any other health condition, talk to your doctor before you start. If you feel unwell at any point, eat, rest and get in touch.
